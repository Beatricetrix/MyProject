Lets prepare omo tuo

This is how to prepare omo tuo from start to finish.

Ingredients

  • 3 cup of broken rice
  • 2 tablespoons of salt
  • 5 cups of water

Instructions

  1. Rinse the broken rice in cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a large pot, combine the rinsed rice and water.
  3. Bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and cover the pot.
  5. Simmer for about 20-25 minutes or until the rice is tender and the water is absorbed.
  6. Remove from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes.
  7. Use a wooden spatula to mash the rice to the walls of the pot.
  8. Continue mashing until the rice becomes smooth and sticky.
  9. Wet your hands with water and shape the mashed rice into small balls.
